About this policy

Qist Customer (pk.qist.customer), provided by Ubaid Badar, lets registered customers view their retailer installment plans and payments.

Sign-in and recovery

Your phone number and password are sent over HTTPS to the Qist backend to sign in. Setting or resetting a password uses a WhatsApp code delivered through Meta. Passwords and codes are not saved by this app.

Account information

The app retrieves your name, registered phone number, available plans, installment dates and amounts, payment receipts, retailer contact details and last acknowledged device lock state. The backend limits access to records matching your verified phone at active retailers. No advertising or analytics SDK is included.

Local storage

A session token is encrypted using an Android Keystore key and kept in storage excluded from Android backup. Privacy acceptance and local preferences remain on this device. Remote push notifications are not available in this version.

Receipts and contact actions

Saving a receipt uses Android's file picker. Sharing a receipt sends its text to the destination you select. Calling a retailer opens the dialer; WhatsApp opens the retailer's WhatsApp link. Those services follow their own privacy policies.

Retention and choices

Logging out revokes the current server session and removes this device's session token. Exported receipts remain until you delete them. Retailers retain contract and payment records; contact your retailer or the privacy contact below for correction or deletion requests and information about applicable retention requirements.

Phone status and payments

This customer app displays the last acknowledged phone lock state and retailer payment guidance. It does not manage the device or process payments. Payment records are entered by your retailer.
